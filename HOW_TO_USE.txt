HOW TO USE: recording-chain-measurement-ingest
====================================

PURPOSE
  Extract objective acoustic ground truth via Praat, Parselmouth, and librosa, while
  decoupling recording chain bias, microphone polar patterns, proximity effect, and room impulse response (RIR).

CAPABILITIES
  - Ingest raw audio files and extract empirical F0, jitter, shimmer, HNR, and formants
  - De-bias microphone proximity effect (low-frequency boost below 200 Hz)
  - Compensate for transducer polar patterns and microphone frequency curves
  - Estimate room reverberation time (RT60 in ms) and apply de-reverberation
  - Maintain forensic confidence ledger tagging every metric as [EMPIRICAL] or [ESTIMATED]
  - Output machine-readable measurement_ingest.json

USE CASES
  - "Don't bake the echoey conference room into this speaker's biometric voice profile"
  - You have an uncalibrated YouTube clip and need to isolate the biological voice from the mic
  - You need objective, measured Praat numbers to populate voice-biometric-profiler
  - You want to separate proximity-effect bass boost from actual vocal cord fundamental

IDEAS
  - Run this skill FIRST whenever you have audio recordings of the subject
  - Feed the de-biased empirical numbers directly into voice-biometric-profiler §§1-4
  - Pass the baseline measurements to clone-verification-match-score as the ground truth

PROMPTS YOU CAN PASTE
  /measurement-ingest path/to/speech_sample.wav

  ingest audio measurements for this recording

  calibrate recording chain of this audio file

  extract acoustic ground truth from this speech sample. Compensate for mic proximity and room RT60.

  Separate room tone and microphone bias from the biological voice in this clip.

EXAMPLE FLOW
  You:   /measurement-ingest rally_audio_clip.wav
  Model: 1. Empirical Measurements:

         - Formants: F1=578, F2=924, F3=2345, F4=3480 Hz
         2. Chain Compensation:
         - Tight dynamic mic detected: -2.5 dB proximity correction below 180 Hz
         3. Room Acoustics:
         - RT60 estimated at 280ms (de-reverberation filter applied)
         4. Confidence: F0/Formants: EMPIRICAL_HIGH; Subglottal pressure: INFERRED
         [measurement_ingest.json]

DEFAULT GOALS IF YOU DON'T NAME ONE
  1. Extraction of F0, perturbation (jitter/shimmer), HNR, and formants F1-F4
  2. Transducer proximity effect and frequency compensation
  3. Room reverberation (RT60) estimation and de-biasing
  4. Machine-readable measurement_ingest.json block
